## About Me

Hello, I’m **Ji Yong-Hyeon**.

I am passionate about **mathematics**, **cryptography**, and the engineering of secure systems. My academic and technical interests center on the design, analysis, and implementation of cryptographic algorithms, with an emphasis on connecting rigorous theory to practical applications.

I am particularly interested in:
- **Cryptographic system design**
- **Algorithm implementation and validation**
- **Mathematical foundations of modern cryptography**
- **Security engineering for real-world environments**

My goal is to contribute to both the **theoretical** and **applied** sides of cryptography by building reliable, efficient, and well-analyzed solutions. Through continuous study, experimentation, and project work, I aim to deepen my expertise and produce work that is both technically sound and practically meaningful.

If you are interested in **cryptography**, **mathematics**, or **research collaboration**, feel free to connect.
